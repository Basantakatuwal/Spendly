Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records, categories, amounts, and dates you log while using the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate and maintain your account, display your expense history and summaries, and improve the functionality of the Service. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's database and is accessible only through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal expense data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted third-party services (such as hosting or font providers) that are necessary to operate the Service, and these providers only process data as needed to perform their function.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
